# Serendib Local: proposed launch offer

Research checked 14 September 2026. These are transport planning rates, not hotel-inclusive packages or approved cost-based quotations.

## Price comparison

| Vehicle | Sri Lanka Private Transfer published from rate | Proposed Serendib Local rate | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sedan | USD 65/day | USD 59/day | 9.23% lower |
| SUV | USD 85/day | USD 77/day | 9.41% lower |
| Minivan | USD 95/day | USD 86/day | 9.47% lower |

Calculation: round 90% of each benchmark upward to a whole dollar. All prices are for the whole vehicle. The benchmark describes driver, fuel, tolls, parking, and driver meals/accommodation as included. Source: [Sri Lanka Private Transfer, tour driver](https://srilankaprivatetransfer.com/tour-driver).

Additional checks: [Sri Lanka Car and Driver Hire's calculator](https://srilankacaranddriverhire.com/trip-cost-calculator/) displayed average rates of approximately USD 67 for a normal car, USD 90 for an SUV and USD 93 for a minivan when checked. [Sri Lanka Private Driver](https://srilankaprivatedriver.com/how-much-does-it-typically-cost-to-hire-a-private-driver-in-sri-lanka-for-sightseeing/) publishes approximately USD 50–65 for a standard car and USD 70–100 for SUVs/vans. Competitor offers vary, and cheaper offers exist. This is not a claim to be 10% cheaper than every business.

Serendib's proposed allowance is 8 hours and an average of 150 km/day over multi-day hires. Comparable time/distance caps were not established for every competitor. Seasonal dates, exact model, luggage, return mileage, taxes, inclusions and availability must be compared in a written quote. Do not advertise a guaranteed like-for-like saving.

Before accepting these prices, calculate fuel and empty return mileage, driver pay, driver meals/lodging, tolls/parking, vehicle hire or depreciation/maintenance, insurance, applicable taxes/payment fees, contingency and profit. The owner has not supplied those costs or approved the provisional rates.

Adapt the route to flight times and seasonal conditions; an extra airport night can be sensible.

## Services at launch

Offer requests for airport/point-to-point transfers, private day drives and multi-day car-and-driver journeys. SUV/minivan requests remain explicitly subject to arrangement. Exact driver, vehicle, luggage capacity and any child-seat/accessibility requirement must be checked before accepting a booking. No fleet ownership is claimed.

Do not sell included accommodation, safaris, whale watching, adventure/water activities, guaranteed train seats or attraction guiding until appropriate providers, operating requirements and costs have been confirmed. The site permits a conversation about optional requests without promising delivery. It makes no historical-experience, rating, award, team-size or review claims.

Airport/point-to-point transfers are quote-only: a single low starting price for unrelated destinations would be misleading. There is no online payment or automatic reservation. Exact payment and cancellation conditions accompany the written quote before acceptance.
